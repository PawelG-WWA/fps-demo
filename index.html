40 FPS on 120 Hz

At 25 ms per frame, 40 FPS divides evenly into 120 Hz (3 refreshes) but forces 3:2 judder on 60 Hz.

Interpolation Buffer

Synthesizing frames requires holding back real frames first, adding 67 ms+ of unavoidable pipeline lag.

Slow-Mo Inspector

At 0.25× speed, 120 Hz refreshes slow to 30 ticks/s so even a 60 Hz screen shows exact cadence.

Budget & Spikes

All modes pay the same scene cost, so 30 FPS absorbs 30 ms spikes (try Load Spike) that make 40 and 60 FPS hitch.
